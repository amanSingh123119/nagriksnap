Awareness & Civic Reporting Guidelines

1. Clear Photo Evidence

Always capture the issue during daytime or with sufficient lighting. Step back a few meters so nearby landmarks or street signs are visible in the background for rapid municipal verification.

2. Accurate Geo-Location

Click "Use GPS" while on site. This embeds exact latitude and longitude into your report, allowing field workers to navigate directly to the exact manhole or broken street pole.

3. Direct Department Routing

Selecting the correct category (e.g. Roads, Water, Electricity) routes your report instantly to the nodal engineer without delays from manual sorting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a complaint to be assigned?

Reports are triaged by AI and assigned to the nodal municipal engineer within 2 to 4 business hours.

What happens if an issue is marked resolved but work is incomplete?

Citizens have 7 days to reopen an issue by providing verification feedback if the physical work was unsatisfactory.
