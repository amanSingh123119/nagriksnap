How it works

A transparent path from concern to follow-up

The platform is designed to help stakeholders coordinate. It does not guarantee that every report will be accepted, funded or resolved.

1

Share the concern

A citizen signs in and submits an issue with useful details and evidence.

2

Review and route

Authorized staff review the report and route it according to their responsibilities.

3

Build a response

Where appropriate, universities and industry partners can propose solutions or support.

4

Track the outcome

Stakeholders update progress and review evidence before an outcome is reported.

About NagrikSnap

Make community challenges easier to surface and coordinate.

NagrikSnap is a civic-tech project concept built around a simple idea: useful community problem-solving requires more than a report form. Citizens need a way to raise concerns; responsible authorities need a way to review them; and universities and industry partners may contribute expertise, technology or resources when a suitable collaboration is approved.

The platform aims to support clearer ownership, traceable updates and cross-stakeholder coordination while keeping access to personal and case information restricted to authorized users.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I browse the website without an account?

Yes. Public information such as the homepage and platform overview can be explored without signing in. Submitting reports and accessing private workspaces requires authentication.

Does choosing Government or Admin on the login screen give me those permissions?

No. The selected workspace is only a sign-in context. The server must verify the account's assigned role and permissions before granting access. Privileged accounts should be provisioned through an authorized process.

Does submitting a complaint guarantee that it will be resolved?

No. Reports may require review, additional information, assignment and action by the responsible authority. The platform is intended to support tracking and coordination, not guarantee an outcome.

Are all features and integrations live?

The project is under development. Some features may be implemented in the code while others require configuration or end-to-end testing. Do not treat demo data, illustrative workflows or unverified integrations as live services.

How is personal information handled?

Only share information needed to describe the issue. Before production use, the platform must have a reviewed privacy notice, appropriate access controls, retention and deletion procedures, and secure storage configured for its deployment.
